# Predicting a directly carried octet in a withheld EVS slot

The test input is one locally held-out two-minute IQ interval. Select every original CRC-valid, consecutive three-phase group in its native MSC output. Reserve the middle slot of each successive three-slot block, giving slots 1, 4, …, 58 (zero-based) and five opportunities at each of the four cycle positions per counter. All 20 central payload values remain outside the predictor across the recording. Use the earliest complete native group as the primary even if it has zero forecasts. No group may borrow a donor from another counter. [Input, receivers and prospective exposure comparison](input-protocol.md)

**Primary rule.** The target is the zero-based payload octet 5; the EVS mode precedes the 32-byte payload. The inputs are the three transmitted mode octets and the immediately preceding and following slots' directly transmitted payload octet 5. When all three modes match and the donor values match, predict that byte. Otherwise abstain. Neither target octets nor CRCs enter the prediction function. The observed packet and DU CRCs select intact original groups before withholding. This measures a statistical guess and leaves the receiver's original erasure policy in effect.

**Baseline.** Use the most frequent target at eligible training centers for the central mode. For a previously unseen eligible mode use the overall most frequent eligible target. Resolve ties by lower byte value. Use the baseline at exactly the same centers. We explored all 21 directly readable offsets on prior recordings and selected offset 5 after seeing their results. The historical 1,102-group member and 81-group Edogawa tables are development data. [Selection sweep and baseline](development.json) · [Trained model](model.json)

Seal the group selections and predictions at a separate revision before comparing with the withheld bytes. A correct full byte requires agreement on all eight bits. Report eligible centers, abstentions, exact and wrong forecasts, matched-bit counts, baseline results on the same slots, and paired disagreements by position. A correct whole-byte guess on this test does not establish a new clear bit at a masked offset. The six-source prior registry is used only after the decisions have been sealed.

**Packet-loss control.** For targets on the same schedule with two same-position donors at slots `s−4` and `s+4` inside the group, withhold the whole packet carrying the target. Give the receiver the group coordinate for this control. It may inspect only fields that survive erasing that packet. Apply the same mode equality and two-donor byte equality conditions. Count physically possible targets, issued forecasts, correct and wrong forecasts and abstentions by position. Associate the true byte only after sealing this separate set of predictions. A hypothetical packet erasure has a native target value available for scoring. A naturally missing source packet needs an independent receipt to supply a label. Report the two circumstances separately.
