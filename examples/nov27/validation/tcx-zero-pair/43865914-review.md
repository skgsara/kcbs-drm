# What the second-position search established at 43865914

The group member reported roughly **37.7 billion** candidate checks through 08:00 Beijing time on November 28. Six priority prefixes were exhausted under their test. Other prefixes were searched in part, and none of the candidates checked matched all 15 second-position words. The update gives no complete execution log or build identifier for that search. Its counts are contributor-reported. The six remaining payload octets and the programme audio for this group are still unverified.

## Checking the validator on the same transmission

The receiver supplement includes a native checker that reads decoded quantized TCX spectrum coefficients and residual bits, serializes them again, and compares the result bit for bit with the corresponding spectrum region. It supports a zero-difference comparison without converting audio back into EVS. The supplement's basic demonstration only requires the checker to emit a trace. We also tested the stronger requirement of an exact match, clear bad-frame and bit-error flags, and a trace for every word.

At this counter the 15 first-position words already have text-CRC-supported labels. We supplied them in chronological order with the same preceding history for each run. We then deliberately changed the same bit in each of those 15 words. Payload offsets exclude the mode and start at zero.

| Input at the first position of 43865914 | Words matching the supplied labels | Words passing the spectral check |
|---|---:|---:|
| Original words | 15/15 | 15/15 |
| Low bit of byte 24 flipped | 0/15 | 1/15 |
| Low bit of byte 27 flipped | 0/15 | 9/15 |
| Low bit of byte 30 flipped | 0/15 | 15/15 |

Byte 30 falls inside the compared spectrum region in all 15 cases, and the decoded audio changed in all 15 slots. The same fixed byte-30 change also passed in all 15 words at each of five other control counters. This gives an explicit wrong value that the available test can accept across a whole position. A future 15/15 candidate would still need independent evidence for bits that survive this check. The handoff's checker and the member's overnight executable have not been shown to be identical. The portable replay preserves all 3,660 slots up to and including the target, so another group member can run the same controls with their own checker. Tracing the known first-position words through the stock decoder explains why the change can pass. In eight words the low bit of byte 30 chooses the sign of a nonzero spectrum coefficient. In the other seven it refines the value of a nonzero coefficient. The decoder consumes both possible values on each of these paths. We observed the same per-word operation and coefficient location in a continuous replay and after individual cold resets. The assignments depend on the rest of the word. [Per-cycle coefficient locations, bit values and reproducible observer](spectral-calibration/b255/README.md)

Using the broader unchanged first-position recordings, 44 of 45 eligible groups reached 15/15. Counter **43865918** gave 13 spectrum mismatches and two missing traces. These replays supplied text-supported first-position words and marked the other positions as erasures. Receiver history or the supplied labels can affect this result. The control measures the end-to-end comparison under the available history. [Unchanged baseline and exception coordinates](first-position-spectrum-replay.json)

In a separate stock encoder test, 15 consecutive synthetic TCX20 words reproduced all 2,739 bits of their main spectra. Two other genuine stock outputs, both mode `FE`, had identical neighboring words and differed only at physical bit 255. The complete byte 30 was `1C` in one and `1D` in the other. Each reproduced all 165 bits of its own coded spectrum. ## Two observations available directly from the signal

* **Text timing.** In the separate 13:17 UTC recording on 3205 kHz, all 114 reviewed pairs of record starts separated by 798 data units had identical record headers, weather or other bulletin bodies, and transmitted CRCs. At 0.4 seconds per data unit that is **319.2 seconds**. One weather record at phase 2 of 43865914 also recurs at the same phase of 43865382 and 43866180. This timestamps the text carousel. The spoken programme remains unidentified.
* **Codec mode.** All 15 second-position words are TCX20. Their known prefixes signal 70–122 spectrum coefficient pairs. Fourteen failed the one-pair filler test at the pair-count check and one stopped at the earlier harmonic-mapping check. A separate IGF extension check ends by physical bit 103, before the first unknown bit at 128. Neither case supplies one of this group's missing bits.
