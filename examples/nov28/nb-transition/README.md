# A narrowband ACELP check at payload octet 24

Three unmodified stock EVS words supplied a reproducible case in which other transmitted fields constrain the **low two bits of payload octet 24**. The cases came from a reference encoder driven by synthetic audio. They all require a 13.2 kb/s narrowband ACELP transition with mode octet `13` (hex). The mode contains the full transition tag `011` for a first-subframe pulse followed by a fourth-subframe pulse.

The high nibble of payload octet 7 gives the low four bits of the first pulse position. The top five bits of payload octet 25 give the low five bits of the fourth-subframe pitch index. Both of these octets are visible in the receiver layout. For this stock encoder branch, an odd pitch index must be at least `174 − 2 × pulse_position` and the index cannot exceed 126. Enumerating the permitted integer pitch lags given the two visible fragments yields the possible high bits of that index. Those two bits sit at physical word bits 206 and 207, the low pair in payload octet 24. Numbering starts at the mode MSB. The checker independently verifies the projected formula against the lag enumeration on all 512 possible visible-fragment combinations. The native controls contain 56 distinct full words with this tag across four stock encodes. The remaining 53 admit all four low-pair values from the two visible fragments. All three restrictive cases were generated by the public 480-second artificial stimulus. The other controls comprise three words from a separate synthetic input and 19 from reference encodes of non-broadcast music and speech. A cycle position in this table comes from the source slot modulo four. [Synthetic stimuli](../../nov27/validation/late-payload/gsc-control/stimuli.py)

**On-air applicability.** In ten published DU archives, 1,214 distinct CRC-checked DUs across 479 counters supplied 24,280 slots. None had mode `13`. The test therefore verifies zero on-air bits at payload octet 24 in these captures. It supplies no p27 check and makes no prediction for the TCX20 or GSC examples. This was a retrospective coverage check performed after the November 19 holdout. [Source hashes and coverage](public-census.json)

From this directory, run `python check_nb.py` for the stock vectors and `python check_air.py` for the on-air census. Optionally pass `--g192 mixed480 /path/to/stream.192` to the stock checker. It verifies the full stream digest, the slot addresses and all 34 words that carried this mode in that encode.
