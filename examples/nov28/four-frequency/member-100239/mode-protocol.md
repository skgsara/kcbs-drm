# Frozen mode-octet / AM programme-timing control

Candidate data are a continuous 300 s 6100 kHz demodulated AM track and the paired 6140 kHz DRM packet snapshot. The reported recording date is November 28 at 02:02:39 UTC. No candidate AM samples or EVS modes have been scored in writing this protocol. The archival calibration uses independent AM, packet presence and directly carried EVS signalling at 11:05 UTC November 27. The already reported −3.12 s archival lag is taken as given. No G.192 words, masks, decoded DRM waveforms, text or reference-selected bytes enter this analysis.

* Read the one mode octet for each physically present packet cell. Slot center is 20 ms × (slot + 0.5). Use the published first complete counter as the 11:05 clock origin. For the 300 s whole-stream snapshot, use its earliest tick having at least one received packet as nominal slot zero. This origin has no externally verified RF timestamp. A single offset over ±12 s can absorb alignment error within that range. Larger uncertainty and insufficient coverage yield inconclusive results. No clock drift fitting, separate fits per block, station-dependent EQ or additional spectra.
* On mono AM, use a 40 ms Hann window at 20 ms centers. Nine bands have edges 200, 350, 500, 700, 950, 1250, 1650, 2150, 2800, 3400 Hz. Compute log mean spectral power in each band with floor 1e-12 on unit-amplitude PCM. Subtract the nine-band frame mean to get relative spectral shape. Do not use signal loudness, decoded digital speech or received payloads.
* Use old DRM nominal times [8,98) for training, at the known lag. Estimate a mean spectral shape for each exact mode, shrunk towards the mean of its top-five-bit group by 20 frame observations. Require the top-five group to have 20 training observations to emit a template. Freeze all templates after this one fit. Within each full 200 ms bin starting on an integer multiple of 0.2 s, average predictions and their corresponding AM shapes across the same received, trained-mode slots. Require at least five pairs per bin. The score is mean Pearson correlation of the nine band columns across bins. A constant column scores zero. Require at least 65% of possible bins and 60 qualified bins in each complete scored interval, and 25 qualified bins in each half. Halves split elapsed time.
* Test the original at the archival lag on nominal times [120,210). Displace the paired held-out AM bin sequence cyclically by every integer step between 10% and 90% of the qualified bins. The pass gate is a score >=0.16, both temporal halves >=0.08 and >=0.04 above the 99th percentile of these displacements. Require an additional negative comparison on November 19 6100 AM for the old queries [120,145) and [175,200): align their first nominal centers to seconds 1 and 30 of the unrelated track. Both interval controls must have 65% and 60 bins. The mean of the two original true-lag scores must exceed the mean of the two date-displaced scores by >=0.05.
* If archival calibration fails any gate, abstain from the new material. If it passes, use the frozen templates in the new pair. Fit one lag on nominal [12,51) in 20 ms steps from −12 to +12 s, taking maximum score and preferring smallest absolute offset then most negative in exact ties. Require training score >=0.16 and the selected lag to be at least 0.2 s from a search boundary. Test on [76,114), safely inside the first 126 AM seconds even after shifting, and at the same lag on continuation [138,201), [225,287). Each block uses the identical absolute, split-half, nonlocal displacement and coverage gates above. An exact word-bit or programme identity conclusion is outside the measurement. Report every scored block at the original lag even if it fails. Do not tune or retry on other inputs.
